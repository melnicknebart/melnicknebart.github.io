Research

My research focuses on observational astrophysics, galactic dynamics, and space instrumentation. I am particularly interested in using multi-wavelength data and computational modeling to understand the formation and evolution of stellar systems.

JWST Observations of Star Clusters in M82

Research Intern, Space Telescope Science Institute (STScI) | 2025-2026

Using mid-infrared data from the James Webb Space Telescope (JWST/MIRI), I identified 254 previously undetected embedded star clusters in the starburst galaxy M82. I combined multi-wavelength datasets (optical, near-infrared, and submillimeter) to build a comprehensive census of star clusters and study their formation and evolution.

This work contributes to understanding the full lifecycle of star clusters in extreme galactic environments.

Research note in preparation

Stellar Stream Dynamics in the Milky Way

Undergraduate Researcher, Columbia University | 2025

Analyzed Gaia DR3 data to study the structure and origin of a stellar stream. I modeled orbital evolution using the Gala Python package, performing forward and backward integrations over 2 Gyr within a Milky Way potential.

I compared simulated morphologies from star cluster and dwarf galaxy progenitor models to observational data, helping constrain the likely origin of the stream.

Microgravity Simulation via 3D Clinostat

Research Assistant (Engineering), Columbia Space Initiative | 2024–Present

Contributed to the design and construction of a 3D clinostat to simulate microgravity conditions for biological and material science experiments. I worked on optimizing rotational mechanisms and conducted performance testing.

This improved simulation accuracy by approximately 20%, enhancing the reliability of ground-based microgravity experiments.

Dual Active Galactic Nuclei (AGN) Study

Research Assistant, Columbia Physics Scholars Program | 2024

Analyzed over 500 GB of Chandra X-ray Observatory data to identify X-ray counterparts to gravitational wave sources. This work contributed to studies of supermassive black hole mergers and dual AGN systems.
